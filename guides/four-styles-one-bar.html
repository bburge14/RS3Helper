RS3 Combat Architect — Field Reference

Four Styles, One Bar

A general-purpose PvM/slayer/bossing loadout for every combat style — Necromancy, Magic, Ranged, and Melee — each built for both Revolution++ and Revolution Basics + manual play.

Scope: these are style-general bars, not tuned for one specific boss's phase mechanics. For a fight with hard enrage timers, damage caps, or reflect phases, tell me the target and I'll re-order the bar around it.
Note on precision: Revolution's internal left-to-right priority and ability availability shift with Jagex balance passes — cross-check the exact order against your in-game ability book before a serious kill.
